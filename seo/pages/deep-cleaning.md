# Deep cleaning

A top-to-bottom reset that reaches what a regular clean skips. Every cleaner listed here is independent — they set their own rate, run their own business, and you pay them directly. HomeKey Clean charges clients nothing.

{{CLEANER_CARDS}}

## What's typically included

- Inside the oven, refrigerator and cabinets
- Baseboards, door frames and switch plates
- Window tracks, sills and interior glass
- Grout, tile and shower door scale
- Behind and under movable furniture
- Light fixtures, vents and ceiling fans

Scope varies by cleaner. Confirm the list above with whoever you book rather than assuming it.

## What's usually not included

- Exterior windows above ground floor
- Carpet shampooing and steam extraction
- Mould remediation
- Anything requiring a ladder over 6ft

Some cleaners will do these as extras. Ask before the visit, not during it.

## How long it takes

4-8 hours for most homes. Worth booking before guests arrive, after construction dust, at the start of a recurring arrangement, or once or twice a year on top of regular visits.

## What to ask before booking

1. How long do you estimate for a home this size, and is that one person or two?
2. Do you bring your own vacuum and supplies, or use mine?
3. Is the oven interior included or priced separately?

## Common questions

**How much does deep cleaning cost?**

There's no set price, because there's no company setting one. Each cleaner sets their own hourly rate and you pay them directly. Ask for an estimate of hours for your specific home — that, times their rate, is your real cost.

**How do I find someone who actually does deep cleaning?**

Filter the directory by the deep cleaning speciality. Cleaners choose their own specialities, so you're seeing what they say they do best.

**Do I pay HomeKey Clean anything?**

No. Clients pay nothing. Cleaners pay for their listing, which is the whole business model.
