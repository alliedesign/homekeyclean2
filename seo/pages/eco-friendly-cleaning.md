# Eco-friendly and low-tox cleaning

Cleaners who work without harsh chemicals, by choice and by request. Every cleaner listed here is independent — they set their own rate, run their own business, and you pay them directly. HomeKey Clean charges clients nothing.

## What's typically included

- Plant-based and fragrance-free products
- Microfibre and steam where it does the job
- Refillable containers, less single-use plastic
- Products safe around children and pets
- Ventilation-conscious methods

Scope varies by cleaner. Confirm the list above with whoever you book rather than assuming it.

## What's usually not included

- Guaranteed disinfection to clinical standard without registered disinfectants
- Removing set stains that need solvents
- Certification claims — ask each cleaner what they actually use

Some cleaners will do these as extras. Ask before the visit, not during it.

## How long it takes

Same as a comparable standard clean. Ask for the actual product list rather than accepting 'green' as a description — the word isn't regulated and means different things to different people.

## What to ask before booking

1. Which products do you use, specifically? Can you send a list?
2. Are you willing to use products I supply instead?
3. How do you handle bathrooms and mould without bleach?

## Common questions

**How much does eco-friendly cleaning cost?**

There's no set price, because there's no company setting one. Each cleaner sets their own hourly rate and you pay them directly. Ask for an estimate of hours for your specific home — that, times their rate, is your real cost.

**How do I find someone who actually does eco-friendly cleaning?**

Filter the directory by the eco-friendly cleaning speciality. Cleaners choose their own specialities, so you're seeing what they say they do best.

**Do I pay HomeKey Clean anything?**

No. Clients pay nothing. Cleaners pay for their listing, which is the whole business model.
