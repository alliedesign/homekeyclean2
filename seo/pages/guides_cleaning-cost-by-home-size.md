# How long does a clean actually take?

## Rough starting points

One bedroom, one bath: 2-3 hours standard, 4-5 deep. Two bed, two bath: 3-4 standard, 5-6 deep. Three bed, two bath: 3-5 standard, 6-8 deep. Four bed and up: 5+ standard, and a deep clean is realistically a full day or a two-person job. Treat these as starting points for a conversation, not quotes.

## Bathrooms are the variable

Bathrooms take disproportionately long. A three-bed with one bathroom is a meaningfully shorter job than a three-bed with three. Count bathrooms before square footage when you're estimating.

## What adds time

Stairs and multiple levels. Pets. Clutter that has to be moved before a surface can be reached. Hard water scale. Long gaps since the last clean. Homes where a lot of surfaces are glass, stone or unsealed wood.

## What saves time

Clearing surfaces before the cleaner arrives is the single biggest one — it's the difference between cleaning and tidying, and tidying is the slower of the two. Sorting parking and access in advance saves a real fifteen minutes in the denser cities.


## Common questions

**Is two cleaners for half the time the same price?**

Usually close, sometimes slightly more, and often worth it for a deep clean — a long job done in one stretch beats one that runs out of daylight.

**Why did my quote go up after the first visit?**

Most often because the home needed more than described. A cleaner who revises once, explains why, and then holds steady is being straight with you.
