# Deep clean or standard clean?

## The short version

A standard clean maintains a home that is already at a baseline. A deep clean creates that baseline. If nobody has cleaned behind the fridge in two years, a standard clean will not do it — not because the cleaner is cutting corners, but because it isn't in the scope or the hours.

## How to tell which you need

Run a finger along the top of a door frame. Open the oven. Look at the grout in the shower corner and the tracks of a sliding door. If those bother you, you want a deep clean. If the surfaces you see daily are the problem, a standard clean is enough.

## The usual pattern

One deep clean to start, then standard visits weekly or fortnightly, then a deep clean once or twice a year on top. Most people who are happy with their arrangement long-term are doing some version of this.

## What a deep clean will not fix

Hard water scale that has etched glass, grout that has stained through, carpet that needs extraction, or mould behind a wall. A deep clean reaches neglected surfaces; it doesn't reverse damage. An honest cleaner will tell you which one you have.


## Common questions

**How often do I need a deep clean?**

Once or twice a year alongside regular visits, or before and after big events — moving, renovation, guests staying.

**Can I book a deep clean without committing to recurring visits?**

Yes. Many people book one, then decide.
