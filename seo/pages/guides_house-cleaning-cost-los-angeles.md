# What house cleaning actually costs here

## Why we don't publish an average

Every cleaner on HomeKey Clean sets their own rate. There is no company price list, because there is no company doing the cleaning — you are hiring an independent person and paying them directly. The rates you see in the directory are the rates they set, live. An 'average' would be a number we made up.

## What actually moves a quote

Square footage matters less than people expect. What moves a quote: how many bathrooms (the slowest rooms in any house), stairs and levels, whether pets live there, how long since the last thorough clean, and how much needs moving before a surface can be cleaned. A cluttered 1,200 sq ft flat can take longer than an empty 2,500 sq ft house.

## Hourly versus flat rate

A flat rate protects the cleaner against a home being worse than described, so it's usually padded. Hourly is honest in both directions — but ask for an estimate of hours up front and agree what happens if it runs over. Most disputes come from neither side saying this out loud.

## The first clean costs more

Almost always, and it should. The first visit brings a home to a baseline; later visits maintain it. A cleaner who quotes their recurring rate for a first visit on a home that hasn't been done in a year is either going to rush it or resent it.

## What you pay us

Nothing. Clients pay HomeKey Clean nothing at all. Cleaners pay for their listing. That's the whole business model, and it's why we have no incentive to push you toward a more expensive cleaner.


## Common questions

**Is it cheaper to book weekly or monthly?**

Per visit, weekly is usually cheaper — the home stays closer to baseline so each visit is shorter. Per month you'll spend more in total. Monthly visits cost more each time because more has accumulated.

**Should I tip?**

It's welcome but not expected, and it's between you and them. Since you're paying an independent person directly rather than a company taking a cut, the rate they quoted is already what they earn.

**Do I need to supply products?**

Ask. Some cleaners bring everything, some prefer your vacuum for hygiene reasons, some will use products you supply if you have preferences. It sometimes affects the rate.
