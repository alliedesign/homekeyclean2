# Questions to ask before the first visit

## Before you book

How many hours do you estimate for a home this size and condition? Is that one person or two? What is your rate, and does it change for the first visit? What is not included?

## About supplies

Do you bring your own products and equipment? Will you use mine if I prefer particular products? Do you need me to supply anything at all?

## About access and scheduling

How do you want to handle keys or entry codes? What notice do you need to reschedule, and is there a cancellation fee? What happens if you're ill?

## About the practical stuff

Are you insured? Do you work alone? Is there parking I should arrange, and does travel time factor into what I pay?

## Then say what matters to you

The single most useful thing you can do is name your two or three priorities out loud. Almost every complaint about a cleaner traces back to an expectation nobody said aloud — the shower mattered most and they spent the time on the floors.


## Common questions

**Is it rude to ask all this?**

No. Anyone experienced has answered these many times and will be glad you asked rather than assumed.

**Should we put it in writing?**

A short message confirming rate, hours, date and what's included is enough, and it resolves nearly every dispute before it starts.
