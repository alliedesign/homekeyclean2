# Home organizing and decluttering

Cleaning is removing dirt. Organizing is deciding where things live. Every cleaner listed here is independent — they set their own rate, run their own business, and you pay them directly. HomeKey Clean charges clients nothing.

{{CLEANER_CARDS}}

## What's typically included

- Sorting a defined zone: keep, relocate, donate, discard
- Setting up systems that survive a busy week
- Pantry, wardrobe, garage and paperwork zones
- Labelling and containment
- Bagging donations ready for drop-off

Scope varies by cleaner. Confirm the list above with whoever you book rather than assuming it.

## What's usually not included

- Hauling away large items
- Valuing or selling possessions
- Decisions about what you keep — that stays yours
- Deep cleaning at the same time unless booked for it

Some cleaners will do these as extras. Ask before the visit, not during it.

## How long it takes

3-6 hours per zone, often across several sessions. Expect to be involved for at least part of it. Nobody can decide what matters to you on your behalf, and a good organizer won't try.

## What to ask before booking

1. Do you work alongside me, or on your own while I'm out?
2. How do you handle things you're unsure about?
3. Do you take donations away or leave them bagged?

## Common questions

**How much does home organizing cost?**

There's no set price, because there's no company setting one. Each cleaner sets their own hourly rate and you pay them directly. Ask for an estimate of hours for your specific home — that, times their rate, is your real cost.

**How do I find someone who actually does home organizing?**

Filter the directory by the home organizing speciality. Cleaners choose their own specialities, so you're seeing what they say they do best.

**Do I pay HomeKey Clean anything?**

No. Clients pay nothing. Cleaners pay for their listing, which is the whole business model.
