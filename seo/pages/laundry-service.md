# Cleaning with laundry included

Wash, dry and fold added to a regular visit. Every cleaner listed here is independent — they set their own rate, run their own business, and you pay them directly. HomeKey Clean charges clients nothing.

{{CLEANER_CARDS}}

## What's typically included

- Wash, dry and fold
- Bed linen changed
- Towels rotated
- Ironing if agreed in advance
- Sorting by fabric and temperature

Scope varies by cleaner. Confirm the list above with whoever you book rather than assuming it.

## What's usually not included

- Dry cleaning drop-off unless agreed
- Stain removal guarantees
- Hand-wash delicates unless discussed
- Wardrobe reorganisation

Some cleaners will do these as extras. Ask before the visit, not during it.

## How long it takes

Adds 1-2 hours, much of it unattended. Machine time is mostly waiting, so this works best combined with a longer visit rather than booked on its own.

## What to ask before booking

1. Does laundry time count as billable hours even while the machine runs?
2. Do you fold and put away, or fold only?
3. How many loads can you fit into a standard visit?

## Common questions

**How much does laundry service cost?**

There's no set price, because there's no company setting one. Each cleaner sets their own hourly rate and you pay them directly. Ask for an estimate of hours for your specific home — that, times their rate, is your real cost.

**How do I find someone who actually does laundry service?**

Filter the directory by the laundry service speciality. Cleaners choose their own specialities, so you're seeing what they say they do best.

**Do I pay HomeKey Clean anything?**

No. Clients pay nothing. Cleaners pay for their listing, which is the whole business model.
