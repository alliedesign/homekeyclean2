# Move-out and move-in cleaning

The clean that decides whether a deposit comes back. Every cleaner listed here is independent — they set their own rate, run their own business, and you pay them directly. HomeKey Clean charges clients nothing.

{{CLEANER_CARDS}}

## What's typically included

- Every cabinet and drawer, inside and out
- Oven, refrigerator, dishwasher interiors
- All appliance exteriors and behind them where movable
- Closets, shelving and storage
- Bathroom scale, grout and fixtures
- Floors throughout after furniture removal

Scope varies by cleaner. Confirm the list above with whoever you book rather than assuming it.

## What's usually not included

- Rubbish and furniture removal
- Patching walls or paint touch-ups
- Carpet replacement
- Garage or exterior unless agreed

Some cleaners will do these as extras. Ask before the visit, not during it.

## How long it takes

3-8 hours depending on size and condition. Book for after the movers leave and the place is empty — cleaning around boxes costs more and works less well. Give yourself a day of slack before the final inspection.

## What to ask before booking

1. Do you photograph the property before and after? (Useful if a deposit is disputed.)
2. Can you work after the movers finish, or do you need the place empty first?
3. What happens if the landlord's inspection flags something you cleaned?

## Common questions

**How much does move-out cleaning cost?**

There's no set price, because there's no company setting one. Each cleaner sets their own hourly rate and you pay them directly. Ask for an estimate of hours for your specific home — that, times their rate, is your real cost.

**How do I find someone who actually does move-out cleaning?**

Filter the directory by the move-out cleaning speciality. Cleaners choose their own specialities, so you're seeing what they say they do best.

**Do I pay HomeKey Clean anything?**

No. Clients pay nothing. Cleaners pay for their listing, which is the whole business model.
