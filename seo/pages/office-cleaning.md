# Office and commercial cleaning

Small offices, studios and retail, cleaned around your hours. Every cleaner listed here is independent — they set their own rate, run their own business, and you pay them directly. HomeKey Clean charges clients nothing.

{{CLEANER_CARDS}}

## What's typically included

- Desks, shared surfaces and meeting rooms
- Kitchen and break areas
- Restrooms and restocking
- Floors, entrances and glass doors
- Bins and recycling
- High-touch points

Scope varies by cleaner. Confirm the list above with whoever you book rather than assuming it.

## What's usually not included

- Specialised floor stripping and waxing
- Exterior window cleaning
- Biohazard or post-incident cleaning
- IT equipment interiors

Some cleaners will do these as extras. Ask before the visit, not during it.

## How long it takes

1-4 hours per visit, usually after hours. Most small offices settle on two or three visits a week. Ask about alarm and key handling before the first visit rather than at the door.

## What to ask before booking

1. Are you insured for commercial premises, and can you show a certificate?
2. Can you work outside business hours, and do you charge differently for it?
3. How do you handle keys, alarm codes and after-hours access?

## Common questions

**How much does office cleaning cost?**

There's no set price, because there's no company setting one. Each cleaner sets their own hourly rate and you pay them directly. Ask for an estimate of hours for your specific home — that, times their rate, is your real cost.

**How do I find someone who actually does office cleaning?**

Filter the directory by the office cleaning speciality. Cleaners choose their own specialities, so you're seeing what they say they do best.

**Do I pay HomeKey Clean anything?**

No. Clients pay nothing. Cleaners pay for their listing, which is the whole business model.
