## Common questions

**How much is office cleaning in Los Angeles?**

Each cleaner sets their own hourly rate — there's no company price. Ask for an hours estimate for your home and multiply by their rate.

**How far ahead should I book in Los Angeles?**

End of month is busiest everywhere in the coverage area, especially for move-outs. Mid-month a few days' notice is usually fine.

**Do you take a commission?**

No. Clients pay nothing. Cleaners pay for their listing.
