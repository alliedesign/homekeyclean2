# Cleaning with pets in the home

Cleaners comfortable working around animals, and equipped for the hair. Every cleaner listed here is independent — they set their own rate, run their own business, and you pay them directly. HomeKey Clean charges clients nothing.

{{CLEANER_CARDS}}

## What's typically included

- Hair from upholstery, skirting and corners
- Pet bedding and feeding areas
- Litter tray areas and surrounds
- Odour treatment on hard surfaces
- Products chosen to be safe around animals

Scope varies by cleaner. Confirm the list above with whoever you book rather than assuming it.

## What's usually not included

- Grooming or handling animals
- Accident cleanup on carpet needing extraction
- Enclosure and tank cleaning unless agreed

Some cleaners will do these as extras. Ask before the visit, not during it.

## How long it takes

Add roughly 30-60 minutes over a comparable home without pets. Say what animals you have and how they behave with strangers when you first make contact. It affects both the time estimate and whether a given cleaner takes the job.

## What to ask before booking

1. Are you comfortable with a dog loose in the house, or should I crate them?
2. Do you have a vacuum rated for pet hair?
3. Are the products you use safe around cats specifically?

## Common questions

**How much does pet-friendly cleaning cost?**

There's no set price, because there's no company setting one. Each cleaner sets their own hourly rate and you pay them directly. Ask for an estimate of hours for your specific home — that, times their rate, is your real cost.

**How do I find someone who actually does pet-friendly cleaning?**

Filter the directory by the pet-friendly cleaning speciality. Cleaners choose their own specialities, so you're seeing what they say they do best.

**Do I pay HomeKey Clean anything?**

No. Clients pay nothing. Cleaners pay for their listing, which is the whole business model.
