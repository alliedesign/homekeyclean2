# Weekly and biweekly cleaning

The same person, on a schedule, who learns your home. Every cleaner listed here is independent — they set their own rate, run their own business, and you pay them directly. HomeKey Clean charges clients nothing.

{{CLEANER_CARDS}}

## What's typically included

- Kitchen and bathrooms in full
- Floors vacuumed and mopped throughout
- Surfaces, mirrors and glass
- Beds made or changed if you leave linen out
- Bins emptied and relined
- A rotating extra area each visit

Scope varies by cleaner. Confirm the list above with whoever you book rather than assuming it.

## What's usually not included

- Deep-clean tasks unless scheduled separately
- Laundry unless agreed
- Tidying that requires deciding where things belong

Some cleaners will do these as extras. Ask before the visit, not during it.

## How long it takes

2-4 hours per visit once established. The first visit normally takes longer and costs more than the ones after it — that's the deep clean that gets the home to a baseline. Ongoing visits maintain it.

## What to ask before booking

1. Is the first visit longer and priced differently? (It usually should be.)
2. What happens if I need to skip a week?
3. Will it be you every time, or could it be someone else?

## Common questions

**How much does recurring cleaning cost?**

There's no set price, because there's no company setting one. Each cleaner sets their own hourly rate and you pay them directly. Ask for an estimate of hours for your specific home — that, times their rate, is your real cost.

**How do I find someone who actually does recurring cleaning?**

Filter the directory by the recurring cleaning speciality. Cleaners choose their own specialities, so you're seeing what they say they do best.

**Do I pay HomeKey Clean anything?**

No. Clients pay nothing. Cleaners pay for their listing, which is the whole business model.
